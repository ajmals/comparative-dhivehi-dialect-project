How Dialect Proximity Works

We measure the phonetic edit distance between regional words and Standard Male' Dhivehi. A higher percentage means the words are closer (fewer sound/letter changes), while a lower score reflects phonetic shifts or unique regional vocabulary.

🌱 Ongoing Project Notice: Dialect terms are continuously collected, digitized from linguistic corpora (e.g. Fritz 2002, Swadesh lists), and contributed by native speakers. Missing words and similarity rankings will update live as new entries are merged into the dataset.
